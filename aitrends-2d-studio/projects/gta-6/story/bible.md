# A Proposta do GTA 6 · bible

**Logline:** Você tem 14 anos, é craque no videogame e péssimo na escola (4, 3, 2). A mãe oferece o GTA 6 (quase R$ 450) se você fechar o ano com média 7 em tudo. Você transforma o estudo num jogo — assalto, mira, mapa, chefão — leva um 5 no simulado ("game over"), volta ao checkpoint e fecha com 8, 7,5 e um 7 raspando. A melhor fase não foi abrir o jogo: foi ver aquele sete.
**Tone:** comédia dramática / superação, ritmo de game. Second person: "você" = o garoto.

## Cast (scenes/cast.js)
- **KID** você — red-orange hoodie #d64a33, spiky hair, kid scale. Expressions: sleepy, determined, thinking, worried, joy, smirk.
- **KID_MASK** — same hoodie + black beanie for the heist fantasy.
- **MOM** — purple dress #8a5ad6, bun. Smirk, determined, neutral reading, smile.
- **Friends** — only as coloured avatars (yellow, green, blue) in the chat and game lobby.

## Locations
- **bedroom** (night): TV glow is the key (cool practical), desk lamp warm, skyline window + moon. Props: TV, console, beanbag, desk, notebook, shelf with controller, poster. Weekend variant: morning sun in the window.
- **dining** (interiorWarm): pendant lamp over the table, night window, mom right, kid left.
- **classroom** (interiorCool / golden on the last day): big windows, chalkboard, wall clock, desk rows.
- **hud** (void/neon): game world — neon grid floor, HUD corners, scanlines. Mission fantasy shots.
- **bp**: blueprint/navy cards for the math (sums, 4 weeks, 2 → 7 bars, the 7,0 meter).
- **3D**: night apartment block (s21) and the history book as a low-poly enemy map (s28).

## Motifs
- Grades as stamped numbers: red for 4/3/2/5, green for 8/7,5/7.
- Game language over real life: HUD, NOVA MISSÃO, GAME OVER, CONTINUE?, CHECKPOINT, health hearts.
- The game case (cold open + callback at the end).
- The 7: gold, circled, star on the wall.
