# Japão — Kurozane no Hayato · bible

**Logline:** A chronicle from a vanished empire has one name scraped out eight times. Under UV light it reads Kurozane no Hayato: the lone survivor of Iki (1019) who, with eleven arrows and his father's tachi, killed every general the Empire of Kezhar sent after him — and then its emperor — cutting one mark into his sheath for each. Today only a song, thirty stones and a sheath with eight marks remain.
**Tone:** epic legend / grief / quiet defiance. Narrated in third person (pt-BR). 16:9, 1080p, captions on.
